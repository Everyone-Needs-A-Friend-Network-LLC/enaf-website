How it works

Four simple steps

  1. 1Call ENAF

    Use the phone you already know.

  2. 2Enter your PIN

    Your enrolled number and four-digit PIN help protect your account.

  3. 3Choose what you need

    Choose companionship, Tech Help, a balance check, a purchase, or a callback request.

  4. 4Talk with a real person

    Waiting, menus, and callback requests do not use conversation minutes.

An extra set of caring ears

Friendly conversation can sometimes bring up something that does not sound right.

If a Companion hears a possible concern during a call, they can submit a factual note to an ENAF supervisor for review.

When appropriate, and when the member has approved a trusted contact, a supervisor may send that person a simple text asking them to check in.

ENAF is not an emergency service, law-enforcement agency, financial advisor, or guaranteed scam-prevention service. If there is immediate danger, hang up and call 911.
